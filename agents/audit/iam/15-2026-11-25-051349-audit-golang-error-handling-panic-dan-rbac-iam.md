# Audit & Perbaikan Go Error Handling, Panic/Recover, dan RBAC Fail-Closed IAM

- **Tanggal:** 25 November 2026 WIB (`2026-11-25-051349`)
- **Scope:** Layanan IAM (`backend/services/iam`) beserta batas integrasi IAM pada Gateway (`backend/gateway/internal/{middleware,source_connections,adapter/iam}`).
- **Metodologi:** Audit 5 kategori sejajar berdasarkan standar `/golang-error-handling` dan `/golang-safety` (pembuatan sentinel & pemetaan kode, pembungkusan `%w`/`errors.AsType`/`errors.Join`, aturan *single-handling* & *fail-open* outbox/RBAC, pemulihan panic & *nil-safety*, serta keamanan logging/PII/rahasi config), dilanjutkan perbaikan, penambahan unit regression tests, lint/vet/`govulncheck`, build ulang container Docker Compose, dan pengujian live HTTPS E2E.

- **Perlindungan Circuit Breaker Gateway dari `ScopeOwnerVerification`:** Memperbarui [`resilience.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/gateway/internal/source_connections/resilience.go) agar error gRPC ber-reason `"iam.scope.owner_verification_unavailable"` tidak ikut memicu circuit breaker IAM global di Gateway, dan memetakan eksplisit reason `"iam.scope.owner_verification_unavailable"`, `"iam.rbac.sensitive_reauth_required"`, serta `"iam.role.in_use"` di [`iam_client.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/gateway/internal/source_connections/iam_client.go).

- **Rentang Smallint `redirect_priority` & Pemetaan PG Constraint:** Menetapkan batas atas `MaxRedirectPriority = 32767` di [`role.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/domain/entity/role.go) dan [`mappers.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/repository/role/mappers.go) selaras kolom PostgreSQL `smallint`, menambah sentinel `ErrRoleInUse` + `ErrInvalidRedirectPriority` di [`rbac.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/domain/errors/rbac.go), membungkus transaksi `RoleRepository.Delete` dengan `pgstore.MapError`, serta menambah SQLSTATE `22003` (`numeric_value_out_of_range`), constraint unik token/sesi/recovery code, dan `identity_id_fkey` pada [`errors.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/repository/pgstore/errors.go).
- **Sanitasi `parseOptionalIP` & Pesan Domain:** Mengganti `err.Error()` mentah `netip.ParseAddr` dengan pesan domain aman di [`session/service.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/services/session/service.go), memperbaiki `ValidateUUIDParam` di [`query.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/repository/pgstore/query.go), memperbaiki pewarisan pesan pada [`common.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/domain/errors/common.go) (`WithField` & deduplikasi `DomainError.Error()`), memperkuat `sanitizedDomainMessage` pada [`handler/errors.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/transport/grpc/handler/errors.go), dan membedakan kode validasi `IssueSession` (`"iam.session.mfa_flag_requires_auth_flow"`) dari RPC deprecated `MarkSessionMfaVerified`.
- **Klasifikasi Kerusakan Kunci/Hash Server & Anti-Oracle Identitas:** Mengubah `AESGCMKeyringCipher.Decrypt` ([`cipher.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/pkg/crypto/cipher.go)) dan `decodeArgon2idPHC` ([`password.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/pkg/crypto/password.go)) menjadi `KindUnavailable`, serta menyelaraskan `SetPrimaryIdentity` ([`identity.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/services/iam/services/user/identity.go)) agar menormalkan UUID dan mengembalikan `ErrIdentityNotFound` (anti-oracle lintas user).

Di Gateway, menutup potensi nil dereference & int32 overflow pada [`middleware/session.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/gateway/internal/middleware/session.go), [`middleware/authorizer.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/gateway/internal/middleware/authorizer.go), [`adapter/iam/adapter.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/gateway/internal/adapter/iam/adapter.go), dan [`adapter/iam/mapper.go`](file:///home/muriarfad/Documents/projects/erp-retail/backend/gateway/internal/adapter/iam/mapper.go).
